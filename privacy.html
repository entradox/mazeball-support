MazeBall — Privacy Policy

Effective 8 November 2026

MazeBall collects no data. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no network access. Everything it remembers stays on your iPhone.

What the app stores on your device

  • Levels you have unlocked, the stars you earned, and your best times
  • Your settings: sound, haptics, tilt sensitivity, reduce-motion, and tilt calibration offsets

This is kept in the app's own on-device storage. It is never uploaded, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

What the app does not do

  • No account, sign-in or profile
  • No analytics, crash reporting or telemetry sent off the device
  • No advertising and no third-party SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and no advertising identifier
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, microphone, camera or health data

Motion sensor

MazeBall reads the device's motion sensors to steer the marble. Those readings are used live to move the ball and are never stored or transmitted.

Sharing and selling

Nothing leaves your device, so there is nothing to share, sell or disclose. No service provider processes anything on our behalf.

Children

MazeBall is rated 4+ and is safe for children: it collects no data, shows no ads, and has no links out to the web.

Your control

Settings → Reset Progress clears saved levels, stars and best times. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
