MazeBall Privacy Policy

Effective 20 November 2026 (Version 1.2)

MazeBall stores your game progress and preferences on your iPhone. Optional Game Center, sharing and feedback use the services described below.

Local game data

Unlocked levels, stars, times, Daily history, marble finishes, calibration and settings stay in the app's local storage. Settings → Reset Progress clears game progress. Deleting the app removes this local data.

Motion sensors

The game uses motion readings to steer the marble during play. Readings are processed on the device and are not saved or sent to a service.

Game Center

Settings → Game Center lets you choose to sign in to Apple's gaming service. Game Center processes your gaming profile and, when a matching leaderboard is configured, your campaign time. Apple operates this service under its privacy terms. Solo play and challenge codes work without Game Center.

Sharing

Beat My Time opens the system share sheet. The result image, course, time, invitation code and app link are sent to the destination you choose. Recipients can race the same course.

Feedback

Send Feedback opens your mail application. If you send a message to mazeball@parmanandllc.com, we receive the message, sender address and any attachments you include to respond to your request.

Your choices and retention

Reset Progress removes local game records. Manage your Game Center profile and service settings with Apple. Sharing sends a copy to the recipient you choose, whose service controls that copy. Feedback messages are retained while we handle your request. Contact mazeball@parmanandllc.com to request deletion of your support correspondence.

App privacy

The game has no advertising or third-party analytics SDK. It does not request location, contacts, photo-library, camera or microphone access. Families can manage Game Center and sharing through the device's controls.
