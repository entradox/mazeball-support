MazeBall — Support

How to play

Tilt your phone to roll the steel marble through the rings and drop it into the centre. Each level has a par time; finish within it for three stars.

The marble drifts or steers the wrong way

Recalibrate for how you actually hold the phone: Settings → Recalibrate Neutral Position, then hold the phone the way you play and tap Set Neutral Angle. The first launch shows the same sheet. Use Default selects a 45-degree neutral holding angle.

The marble looks stuck

Tap the restart button in the level HUD. If a level looks impossible, email us with the level number — every level is generated and tested solvable, so we want to know.

No sound or vibration

Check Settings in the app (Sound, Haptics) and your iPhone's silent switch for sound.

My progress disappeared

Progress, stars and best times are stored on your device only — there is no account and no cloud backup. Deleting the app or resetting progress removes them permanently.

Motion sensitivity

Settings → Tilt Sensitivity changes how far you have to tilt. Reduce Motion turns down in-game animation. Recalibrate after changing either.

Requirements

iPhone on iOS 17 or later. MazeBall is a portrait, iPhone-only game.

Challenge a friend

After a finish, tap Beat My Time. A recipient with MazeBall can open the invitation URL. After installing, use Home → Enter Code and enter the code from the invitation. Both players receive the same board and target. Challenge attempts have their own result and leave campaign progress intact.

Daily timing

Daily changes at midnight UTC. A run keeps the course it began with. Best times belong to that course and date. A missed day ends a streak.

Game Center

Open Settings → Game Center to sign in and see your Apple gaming profile. Solo play and code challenges are available offline. Live course rankings require the matching leaderboard setup in the released build.

Contact
